## Overview

WasteChain™ is the FAA.zone Seedwave sector portal for zero-waste and circular economy infrastructure. It provides:

- **Circular Economy Engine** — end-to-end material flow tracking on VaultTrace™
- **GreenChain Compliance** — automated zero-waste certification and regulatory reporting
- **Resource Recovery Marketplace** — match recovered materials with verified buyers
- **Carbon Offset Tokenisation** — convert diversion events into tradable carbon credits
- **EPR Compliance Tools** — producer responsibility, deposit-return, and take-back management
